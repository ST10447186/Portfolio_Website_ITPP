About me

IT student at the IIE, building skills in software development and computer networking.

Why I chose information technology

My journey into IT started with a natural curiosity about what was actually happening behind the screen, wanting to understand the hardware boosting my frame rates and the code driving complex game mechanics. Hours spent troubleshooting setups, tweaking performance settings, and optimizing networks built an intuitive technical foundation before I ever stepped into a classroom. The analytical problem-solving and quick adaptability I developed while gaming seamlessly translated into diagnosing system bugs, writing code, and configuring network architectures. Ultimately, pursuing IT allows me to take my lifelong passion for interactive tech and turn it into a meaningful career where I get to build, secure, and innovate the systems I love.

My career goals

Over the next five years, my goal is to launch and grow my career as a full-stack software engineer, turning my passion for interactive technology and problem-solving into impactful digital solutions. Having built a strong foundation in developing mobile apps, interactive web platforms, and clean back-end logic, I want to take those skills to the next level by building scalable, user-focused applications. I see myself working within dynamic engineering teams where I can design intuitive user experiences, tackle complex system architectures, and continuously adapt to emerging technologies. Ultimately, I want to write clean, efficient code that directly improves how people interact with technology every day.

My interests in IT

I enjoy software development, especially designing Java programs around clear classes and responsibilities. Through my networking studies I have also become interested in how networks are planned, addressed and secured. I am also developing my web development skills, and I am interested in how software and networks can be designed from the start to keep people’s data secure.
